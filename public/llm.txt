# CoinNewsWired - AI Crawler Guide

## Site Information
- **Name**: CoinNewsWired
- **Description**: Leading source for crypto news, Bitcoin price analysis, Ethereum updates, DeFi, NFTs, regulation and blockchain technology
- **URL**: https://coinnewswired.online
- **Language**: en-US
- **Content Type**: Cryptocurrency news, market analysis, educational content

## Content Structure

### Main Sections
- **Home** (`/`): Featured articles, breaking news, latest articles, market analysis, magazine
- **Latest** (`/latest`): All articles with pagination
- **Categories** (`/category/[slug]`): Articles by category
  - Bitcoin (`/category/bitcoin`)
  - Ethereum (`/category/ethereum`)
  - DeFi (`/category/defi`)
  - NFTs (`/category/nfts`)
  - Regulation (`/category/regulation`)
  - Trading (`/category/trading`)
  - Web3 (`/category/web3`)
  - Mining (`/category/mining`)
- **Article** (`/article/[slug]`): Individual article pages with full content
- **Newsletter** (`/api/newsletter`): Newsletter subscription endpoint

### Content Types
1. **News Articles**: Breaking crypto news, market updates, regulatory developments
2. **Market Analysis**: Price analysis, technical analysis, market trends
3. **Educational Content**: Guides, tutorials, explainers
4. **Magazine**: Long-form features, interviews, opinion pieces

## Article Schema
Each article contains:
- **Title**: Headline
- **Excerpt**: Brief summary
- **Content**: Full article body (HTML)
- **Cover Image**: Featured image URL
- **Category**: One of 8 categories with color/icon
- **Author**: Name, avatar, bio, social links
- **Published Date**: ISO 8601 format
- **Updated Date**: ISO 8601 format
- **Read Time**: Estimated reading time in minutes
- **Breaking**: Boolean flag for breaking news
- **Slug**: URL-friendly identifier

## Structured Data
Pages include JSON-LD structured data:
- **NewsArticle** schema on article pages
- **WebSite** schema on homepage
- **Organization** schema site-wide
- **BreadcrumbList** on category and article pages

## SEO Features
- Dynamic sitemap.xml at `/sitemap.xml`
- robots.txt at `/robots.txt`
- RSS 2.0 feed at `/rss.xml`
- Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags
- Canonical URLs
- Semantic HTML5 structure
- Proper heading hierarchy (h1-h3)
- Alt text for all images
- ARIA labels for accessibility

## Crawling Guidelines
- **Respect robots.txt**: Disallows admin, api, private paths
- **Rate Limit**: Be respectful, limit requests
- **Follow sitemap.xml**: For discovering all content
- **Check RSS feed**: For real-time updates
- **Use structured data**: For rich snippet extraction
